Recycle GroupCommunity

Reuse. Redistribute. Recover. Recycle. Landfill last.

Before anything is processed, it is assessed for reuse. Recovered furniture and household essentials go to more than 45 charity and community organisations, large and small, as material aid and support.

Our story, our passion

Recycling isn't always about breaking something down. Sometimes, it's about passing it on.

An older man loses the dog that has shared his life for many years. In the backyard sits a perfectly good kennel, no longer needed but still full of purpose. Across town, a young boy gets his first puppy, but his family cannot afford a kennel of their own.

Instead of becoming waste, that kennel becomes part of someone else's story.

This is the circular economy in its most meaningful form — extending the life of useful items, reducing waste, and helping people within our communities.

Every item is assessed for reuse before it goes anywhere near the processing line.

How it works

Material aid, organisation by organisation.

Charity and community organisations act as the channel: they refer or collect on behalf of people who need the basics of a home. The arrangement is agreed with each organisation — there is no one-size-fits-all program, and no assumption about who pays for what.

What moves

Beds, sofas, tables and chairs, whitegoods, storage, homewares — the things that turn a room into somewhere to live. Recovered from clean-outs and collections, checked, and made available.

Who it reaches

People leaving family violence, people coming out of homelessness, families in hardship, community housing tenants — through the organisations that already support them.

How to join

If your organisation supports people who need household essentials, we'd like to hear from you. Tell us who you support and what they typically need, and we'll work out the arrangement together.
