Recycle GroupAbout

Operators. Not executives.

A working family of businesses led by people who drive the trucks, run the plant, sort the stock and answer the phone. That is why it works, and it is most of what there is to say about us.

What we are

Built backwards from the hard part.

Most waste businesses start with a truck and work out where to tip it. Recycle Group started with the material nobody could process, built the plant for it, and then put the collection brands in front.

That order is the reason the group owns a mattress line, Australia's largest polystyrene plant, a cable granulator, a public transfer station, a 100-acre soils and aggregates site, a reuse channel and the collection brands that feed all of it. Each one was added because a material had nowhere sensible to go.

It is also why the group is unusually direct about the things it can't do. Asbestos and prescribed waste are refused. Residual is published rather than netted off. And nobody is asked to sign anything before they have stood on the floor and watched their own material go through.

North Geelong — where the argument either holds up or it doesn't.
