Why the group exists

Anyone will take a clean load of cardboard.

The material that actually fills landfill is the awkward material: bulky, layered, contaminated, or worth less than the cost of pulling it apart. Recycle Group invested in the plant to take exactly that, and then separated everything else around it.

A mattress is steel, foam, fibre and timber glued into one object. Polystyrene is up to 98% air, so a truckload is worth almost nothing until you densify it. Cable is copper wrapped in plastic. Each one needs its own machine — which is why most operators send them straight to landfill, and why we didn't.

How it works

Nothing here is "away."

Five steps, always in this order. Landfill is where the residual goes, not where the load goes.

01
Collect

JUNK, Trash. and Declutter pick up from homes, businesses, sites and councils.

02
Process

Every load is received at our own facility and sorted into material streams on site.

03
Re-use

Usable goods go to people who need them, through community partners.

04
Recover

Mattresses, polystyrene, cardboard, metal, timber and soils become commodities again.

05
Reduce

What's left is reduced to a quarter of its volume before it goes anywhere near landfill.

Our story, our passion

Recycling isn't always about breaking something down. Sometimes, it's about passing it on.

An older man loses the dog that has shared his life for many years. In the backyard sits a perfectly good kennel, no longer needed but still full of purpose. Across town, a young boy gets his first puppy, but his family cannot afford a kennel of their own.

Instead of becoming waste, that kennel becomes part of someone else's story.
