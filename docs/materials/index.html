Recycle GroupMaterial streams

We don't just talk green.

Every load is received, sorted and streamed into more than 90 categories. These are the streams that matter most — by weight, by volume and by how hard they are to keep out of the ground.

Mattresses

Around 30% of a mattress is steel. Springs are separated by magnets, vibration, high-volume suction and an electrostatic fibre separator, baled 99% clean, and exported as a commodity. Foam and fibre are separated for downstream use.

The Mattress Recycling Company

Polystyrene

Expanded polystyrene is up to 98% air. It is densified on site into solid blocks — a fraction of the volume — and reprocessed into new products such as picture frames, cornices and laminated timber.

Styro

E-waste & cable

Cable is granulated to separate copper from insulation. Electrical items and IT equipment are handled to AS/NZS 5377, with data-bearing devices sanitised to NIST 800-88.

Recycle Group North Geelong

Timber & pallets

Plain timber and pallets are recovered from clean-outs, sites and the transfer station and reprocessed rather than sent to landfill.

Timbercycle

Metal

Ferrous and non-ferrous metal recovered across the site — from whitegoods to spring steel to copper granulate — is baled or containerised and exported as a commodity.

Recycle Group North Geelong

Cardboard

Baled on conveyor-fed balers, containerised and exported. One of the four operations that run from the North Geelong site.

Recycle Group North Geelong

Green waste

Green waste from clean-ups, council collections and the transfer station goes to Nunjara — the group's traceable resource recovery arm — rather than to landfill.

Nunjara

The hard-to-place items

Paint, car, domestic and lithium batteries, NOS canisters, fire extinguishers and tyres are accepted at the transfer station — because the alternative is unlawful dumping. Asbestos, prescribed waste, hazardous chemicals, firearms and explosives are not.

Recycle Group North Geelong

Why recycling costs more than the tip

Recovery is work.

Landfill is cheap because it does nothing. Recovering a mattress means seven operators, two diesel shredders using more than 450 litres of fuel a day each, four dust extraction units that take four hours each to clean, and $8.5 million of plant to recover 99% of the steel. That is why a recycled mattress costs more than a tipped one — and why the group publishes the residual rather than hiding it.

  • Receive and assess. Every item is checked for reuse first.
  • Separate. Magnets, vibration, suction and electrostatic separation split materials cleanly.
  • Bale, densify or containerise. Each stream is prepared as a commodity a manufacturer can use.
  • Recycle back into manufacturing. Steel, polystyrene, cardboard and metal leave as raw material, not waste.
