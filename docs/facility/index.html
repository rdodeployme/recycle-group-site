Recycle GroupThe facility

One site. Four operations.

Recycle Group North Geelong is a true resource recovery centre, designed and purpose-built for landfill diversion. A mattress recycling plant, Australia's largest polystyrene plant, a public resource recovery transfer station, and cardboard containerisation and export — all from 116 Furner Avenue.

Four operations

What runs under one roof.

01

Mattress recycling

The Mattress Recycling Company processes 12,000 to 30,000 mattresses a month — more than half of Victoria's discarded mattresses. Each one is dismantled in about 38 seconds: springs separated by magnets, vibration, high-volume suction and an electrostatic fibre separator; foam and fibre split off; steel baled 99% clean and exported as a commodity.

Plant: 7 operators, 3 excavators, 6 conveyors, 2 vibrating decks, 2 Terex twin-shaft shredders, 3 magnets, 4 dust extraction units and 2 airblades.

02

Polystyrene

Australia's largest polystyrene plant. Expanded polystyrene is up to 98% air; five hot-melt units and one cold press densify 300+ m³ a day into solid blocks — three shipping containers of foam become a cube the size of a washing machine — reprocessed into products such as picture frames, cornices and laminated timber. Licensed for export.

03

Resource recovery transfer station

A public transfer station open to residents and trade, undercover, six days a week. Material is separated into more than 90 streams on site — from timber, metal and green waste to paint, batteries, e-waste, cables and tyres — so the widest possible range of items has a recovery pathway rather than being consigned to landfill.

04

Cardboard containerisation & export

Cardboard is baled on conveyor-fed balers, containerised and exported as a commodity. Metal recovered across the site is exported the same way.

Assurance

Every shift, in checks.

Safety, training and compliance run on eCheck — the group's own system, built in-house. Pre-start checks on plant and vehicles, daily staff declarations, training modules for the mattress and polystyrene lines, incident and fault reporting with manager follow-up, and policies acknowledged by signature.
