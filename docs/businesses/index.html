Recycle GroupBusinesses

A family of brands. One mission.

Every business exists because a specific material, customer or job needed something the market wasn't giving them. Together they close the loop: what one collects, another processes, reuses or recovers.

Collection Getting it out of the house, the site and the street
Rubbish removal & clean-outs

JUNK

Two-person crews, a price before anything is booked, and every load back to the facility. House, moving and estate clean-outs, offices and commercial work, council hard waste. Established in Melbourne and Brisbane.

junk.com.au
Bulky item collection

Trash.

Big stuff. Gone. Online booking for the bulky, heavy and awkward items that won't fit in the car — fridges, washers, mattresses, sofas, trampolines, spas, pianos. Pick the item, flash a photo, see the price, book the pickup.

trash.com.au
Decluttering & re-organising

Declutter

Australia's only rubbish and re-organising company run by women — a service run by women, for women. Based in Geelong.

declutter.com.au
Marketplace · coming 2027

Rubbish.com.au

A rubbish removal marketplace: post photos, get up to three quotes from registered local collectors, choose one and get it gone.

rubbish.com.au
Processing & recovery Turning what comes in back into material
Resource recovery · North Geelong

Recycle Group North Geelong

The group's own resource recovery centre at 116 Furner Avenue — a public transfer station, undercover, six days a week, separating material into 90+ streams on site.

recycle.net.au
Mattress recycling

The Mattress Recycling Company

Mattress collection and processing at scale — 12,000 to 30,000 a month, steel springs recovered as a commodity, foam and fibre separated, and the landfill share cut to the residual.

themattressrecyclingcompany.com.au
Styro
Polystyrene recovery

Styro

Expanded polystyrene collected, densified and reprocessed on site — three shipping containers of foam become a cube the size of a washing machine, ready for manufacture. Licensed for export.

styro.com.au

Timbercycle

Plain timber and pallets recovered from clean-outs, sites and the transfer station and reprocessed rather than sent to landfill.

How they connect

What one collects, another recovers.

The collection brands bring material in. The facility sorts it. Reuse partners and Recycle Warehouse take what still works. The processing brands turn the rest into commodities. Nothing is handed to a third party at the gate.
