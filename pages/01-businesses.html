Why it is built this way

Most operators hand it on.

Collection is a separate industry from processing, and processing is a separate industry from reuse. That is why the chain of custody usually ends at the gate, and why nobody can tell you what happened to your couch.

Recycle Group put all three under one owner. The collection brands feed the facility, the facility feeds the processors and the reuse channel, and the residual is the only thing that leaves for landfill. It is not a tidier org chart — it is the only reason the destinations can be documented.
