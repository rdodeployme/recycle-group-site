Recycle GroupCommunity

Love not landfill.

Before anything is weighed, dismantled or baled, it gets looked at by someone asking a simpler question: could a person still use this? Reuse. Redistribute. Recover. Recycle. Landfill last.

5–10% of mattresses

Every month, between 5 and 10% of the mattresses collected are in good enough condition to be donated rather than dismantled.

We call it The Mattress Project. It is the clearest example of the order the whole group runs in — because a mattress that goes to a family who needed a bed is worth more than the steel in it.

Our story, our passion

Recycling isn't always about breaking something down. Sometimes, it's about passing it on.

An older man loses the dog that has shared his life for many years. In the backyard sits a perfectly good kennel, no longer needed but still full of purpose. Across town, a young boy gets his first puppy, but his family cannot afford a kennel of their own.

Instead of becoming waste, that kennel becomes part of someone else's story.

This is the circular economy in its most meaningful form — extending the life of useful items, reducing waste, and helping people within our communities.

Every item is assessed for reuse before it goes anywhere near the processing line.

How it works

Material aid, organisation by organisation.

Charity and community organisations are the channel. They refer or collect on behalf of people who need the basics of a home, and the arrangement is agreed with each organisation rather than run as one scheme.

What moves

Beds, sofas, tables and chairs, whitegoods, storage and homewares — the things that turn a room into somewhere to live. Recovered from clean-outs, hard waste rounds and collections, checked, and made available.

Who it reaches

Through organisations working with people leaving family violence, people coming out of homelessness, families in hardship, and community housing tenants.

How to join

If your organisation supports people who need household essentials, we would like to hear from you. Tell us who you support and what they typically need, and we will work out the arrangement together.
