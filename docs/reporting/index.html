Reporting

Numbers a board can sign off on.

Every figure comes from a weighbridge ticket, not a conversion table. Councils and commercial partners receive diversion statements they can put in front of an auditor, a councillor or a shareholder.

What you receive
  • 01

    Collection statement

    Tonnes received, tonnes recovered and residual for each collection, by stream.

  • 02

    Site and program diversion reports

    Rolled up by site, region and period, with the diversion rate against the group's 95% target and months below target published as recorded.

  • 03

    ESG and sustainability inputs

    Client-level diversion statements for annual reporting, available to commercial and council partners on request.

Method

How the numbers are produced.

Measured, not modelled

The diversion rate is recovered tonnes divided by received tonnes. No estimates, no conversion factors.

Why we can show it

Collection, transfer, processing and reuse are all in the group, so material stays inside the chain of custody until it becomes something else.

What we exclude

Asbestos and hazardous waste are not accepted and never enter these figures. Residual is reported in full rather than netted off.
