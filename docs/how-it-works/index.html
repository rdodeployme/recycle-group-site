Recycle GroupHow it works

Nothing here is "away."

Every load is received, sorted and streamed. Five steps, always in this order — and landfill is where the residual goes, not where the load goes.

01

Collect

Crews pick up from homes, businesses, building sites and councils. A price is agreed before anything is booked, the crew does the lifting, and every load comes back to the facility rather than to the nearest tip.

JUNKTrash.DeclutterCouncil hard waste
02

Process

Everything is received at Recycle Group North Geelong — undercover, six days a week — and separated into more than 90 material streams on site. Residents and trade use the same transfer station.

Our facilities90+ streamsEPA R000312600
03

Re-use

Before anything is processed, it is assessed for reuse. Usable furniture and household essentials go to people who need them — through more than 45 charity and community organisations.

Community partnersReuse first
04

Recover

Mattresses, polystyrene, cardboard, metal and timber are processed into commodities and recycled back into manufacturing — spring steel baled for export, polystyrene densified, cardboard containerised. Green waste, soils and concrete go to the group's Nunjara site at Moorabool.

Material streamsTMRCStyro
05

Reduce

What can't be reused or recovered is reduced to about a quarter of its original volume before it goes anywhere near landfill — less landfill space, better compaction, and a more stable rehabilitation cell.

Landfill lastResidual reported, not netted off

Why we can say this

One chain, start to finish.

One chain of custody

Most operators collect and hand off, so the chain of custody ends at the gate. Recycle Group owns collection, transfer, processing and reuse retail, so material stays inside the group until it becomes something else.

Sorted and streamed

Every load is received, split at the sorting line and streamed into more than 90 material categories on site. Reporting on a partner's material is agreed as part of the arrangement.

What we exclude

Asbestos, prescribed waste, hazardous chemicals, firearms and explosives are not accepted. What can't be recovered is called residual — not folded into a recovery figure.

Landfill transfers the problem. Recycling transforms it.™
Richard Furnari, Founder & CEO
