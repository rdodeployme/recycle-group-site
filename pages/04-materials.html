The four that matter most

We don't just talk green.

By weight, by volume, and by how hard they are to keep out of the ground, these are the streams the group was built around.

Mattresses

Around 30% of a mattress is steel. Springs are separated by magnets, vibration, high-volume suction and an electrostatic fibre separator, baled 99% clean, and exported as a commodity. Roughly 270 tonnes of steel out of every 10,000 mattresses in.

The Mattress Recycling Company

Polystyrene

Up to 98% air, which is why it is uneconomic for everyone else. Densified on site into solid blocks — a fraction of the volume — and reprocessed into new product. Businesses can order reusable Styro Bags in 1, 4 or 8 m³ and have them collected.

Styro

E-waste & cable

Cable is granulated to separate copper from insulation. Electrical items and IT equipment are handled to AS/NZS 5377, with data-bearing devices sanitised to NIST 800-88 — which is what makes the stream usable for a council or a corporate.

Recycle North Geelong

Green waste, soils & aggregate

Green waste becomes mulch and compost; concrete, brick and rock are crushed into recycled aggregate; contaminated and mixed soils are blended back into usable material. All at the group's 100-acre Moorabool site.

The honest part

Recovery is work.

Landfill is cheap because it does nothing. Recovering a mattress means seven operators, two diesel shredders using more than 450 litres of fuel a day each, four dust extraction units that take four hours each to clean, and $8.5 million of plant to get 99% of the steel out.

That is why a recycled mattress costs more than a tipped one. It is also why what can't be recovered is called residual rather than folded into a recovery figure, and why we would rather show someone the floor than send them a brochure.

See the plantThe five steps
  • Receive and assess. Every item is checked for reuse before anything else happens.
  • Separate. Magnets, vibration, suction and electrostatic separation split materials cleanly.
  • Bale, densify or crush. Each stream is prepared as something a manufacturer can actually use.
  • Back into manufacturing. Steel, polystyrene, cardboard, copper and aggregate leave as raw material, not waste.
