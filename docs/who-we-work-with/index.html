Who we work with

Recycling requirements, from council to boardroom.

Recycle Group runs recycling programs for organisations that have to prove the outcome — councils, corporations, listed companies and the people who manage property and construction sites. The infrastructure is the same; the program is designed around the requirement.

01

Councils & government

Kerbside hard waste, transfer station operation, resource recovery contracts and the diversion evidence your community and auditors expect.

Councils come to Recycle Group for programs that have to run on a schedule, at scale, and produce a number that stands up in a council report. Hard waste collections, bulk clean-ups after events or illegal dumping, transfer station operation and material processing all sit inside the group, so the diversion figure is measured at the weighbridge rather than estimated from a conversion table.

  • Kerbside hard waste programs
  • Transfer station operation
  • Resource recovery contracts
  • Community drop-off and reuse
  • Diversion reporting per program
02

Corporations & multi-site operators

Recycling programs across offices, warehouses, retail and logistics networks — one contract, one reporting standard, every site.

Multi-site businesses need one program that behaves the same way at every site: the same streams, the same collection standard, the same report. Recycle Group designs the program once, runs the collections with its own crews and fleet, processes the material in its own facilities and reports by site, region and period.

  • Office, warehouse and retail clearances
  • Scheduled collections across a network
  • Fit-out and end-of-lease programs
  • Site-level and portfolio reporting
  • Single contract, single point of contact
03

ASX-listed companies

Measured diversion, chain of custody and reporting that stands up in a sustainability report, an ESG disclosure or a board paper.

Listed companies are reporting recycling outcomes to shareholders, and those numbers get questioned. Because collection, transfer, processing and reuse all stay inside the group, Recycle Group can show where the material went, what it became and what went to landfill — with residual published in full rather than netted off.

  • Chain of custody from site to commodity
  • Diversion statements for annual reporting
  • Residual reported in full
  • Data-bearing and sensitive material handled on request
  • Reporting cadence agreed with the program
04

Property, facilities & construction

Clearances, fit-out and strip-out programs, end-of-lease work and site material recovery, with the tonnes accounted for.

Property and facilities managers deal in deadlines: a tenancy ends, a fit-out starts, a site has to be clear by Monday. Recycle Group clears the space, separates the material into more than 90 streams at the resource recovery centre, and hands back a statement of what was recovered — useful for the client, the builder and the building's own reporting.

  • Strip-out and fit-out waste
  • End-of-lease and vacant possession clearances
  • Timber, metal, cardboard and plasterboard recovery
  • Furniture and fittings reuse where possible
  • Collection statements per job
