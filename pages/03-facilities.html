North Geelong

One site. Four operations.

A true resource recovery centre, designed and purpose-built for landfill diversion — a mattress recycling plant, Australia's largest polystyrene plant, a public resource recovery transfer station, and cardboard containerisation and export, all from one address.

01

Mattress recycling

12,000 to 30,000 mattresses a month — more than half of Victoria's discarded mattresses. Each one is dismantled in about 38 seconds: springs separated by magnets, vibration, high-volume suction and an electrostatic fibre separator; foam and fibre split off; steel baled 99% clean and exported as a commodity.

Plant: 7 operators, 3 excavators, 6 conveyors, 2 vibrating decks, 2 Terex twin-shaft shredders, 3 magnets, 4 dust extraction units and 2 airblades.

02

Polystyrene

Australia's largest polystyrene plant, run as Styro and operating for more than 25 years. Five hot-melt units and one cold press densify 300+ m³ a day into solid blocks, reprocessed into picture frames, skirting boards, wall panels, outdoor furniture and packaging. The group holds a waste export licence for expanded polystyrene, including densified and reprocessed (WASTE-LIC-2026-V6M6D7). Businesses can order reusable 1, 4 or 8 m³ Styro Bags and have them collected.

03

Public transfer station

Open to residents and trade six days a week, fully undercover. Free drop-off for whitegoods, clean scrap metal, cardboard and polystyrene up to half a cubic metre, printer cartridges, selected e-waste, architectural paint up to 100 litres and car batteries. A resident discount applies across Geelong, Queenscliff, Surf Coast, Golden Plains, Wyndham and Colac Otway.

04

Cardboard containerisation & export

Cardboard is baled on conveyor-fed balers, containerised and exported as a commodity. Metal recovered across the site leaves the same way.
